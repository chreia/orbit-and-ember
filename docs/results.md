From 2011 to 2012 a naive merge of every FIRMS sensor rises 3.1-fold because VIIRS begins. The harmonized record changes by -15%, in line with MODIS on its own (-20%), so the sensor step is gone.

## Uncertainty

The 80% interval for model months covers 81% of held-out region-months in the backcast test and 80% in leave one year out.

The transfer model undercounts slightly on held-out years (total bias -3% leaving one year out, -7% in the backcast), so reconstructed years before 2012 may read a few percent low; the interval is built from those same held-out errors.
