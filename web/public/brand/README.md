# NASA Space Apps logos

Official 2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge logos, unmodified, from the brand page
(https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/brand/, "Download NASA Space Apps Logos").

Rules from the NASA Space Apps Brand Guidelines that apply here:

- Use the files as they are: do not recolour, stretch, rotate, skew, outline or rearrange them.
- The default (horizontal) logo is preferred; the small mark is for tight spaces such as icons.
- Keep clear space around the logo and do not place it on busy imagery.
- Do not use NASA's insignia or logotype, and do not imply that NASA endorses this project.
